How Technology Helps

Communication Tools

Picture-based and text-to-speech apps help non-speaking or minimally speaking people express themselves.

Learning and Routine Apps

Visual schedules, reminders and educational apps help build daily routines and learning skills.

Accessible Web Design

Calm colors, readable fonts, clear layouts and no flashing content make websites easier for neurodiverse users. This website follows these principles.

AI and Data

Researchers use data analysis and AI to study early signs of autism and to build personalized learning support.
