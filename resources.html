Support and Resources

For Families

  • Keep a clear daily routine
  • Use simple, direct language
  • Celebrate small achievements
  • Connect with other families for support

For Teachers

  • Use visual schedules and clear instructions
  • Provide a quiet space when needed
  • Give extra time for tasks and transitions

For Employers

  • Give clear, written task descriptions
  • Offer a comfortable work environment
  • Focus on strengths such as attention to detail

Trusted Organizations

  • World Health Organization: Autism fact sheet
  • Autism Speaks
