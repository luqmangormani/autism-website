What is Autism?

Autism, or Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), is a difference in how the brain develops. It affects how a person communicates, interacts with others, and experiences the world. It is called a spectrum because it looks different in every person.

Common Signs

  • Difficulty with eye contact or social conversation
  • Strong interest in specific topics
  • Preference for routines and sensitivity to change
  • Sensitivity to sound, light or touch

What is Neurodiversity?

Neurodiversity means that brain differences, such as autism and ADHD, are natural variations of human minds and should be respected and supported.

Myth vs Fact

Myth: Autism is caused by bad parenting.

Fact: Autism is not caused by parenting. Research points to genetic and developmental factors.

Myth: Autistic people cannot succeed in work or study.

Fact: With the right support, many autistic people do very well in education and careers.

Myth: All autistic people have the same abilities.

Fact: Every autistic person is different, with their own strengths and challenges.
